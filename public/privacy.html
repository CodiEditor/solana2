Privacy

Updated 17 October 2026

What the server stores

Your wallet address, your credit balance, your count of accepted shares, the times your wallet first connected and was last seen, the credits you earned today (for the daily limit), and a short list of recent accepted shares with address, time and credits. Sign-in messages and mining challenges are kept in memory for a short time and then dropped.

What the leaderboard shows

The leaderboard is public. It shows a shortened address (the first four and last four characters), shares and credits.

What your browser sends

Your wallet address, the signature of the sign-in message, and mining proofs (a nonce, a hash and a challenge). AetherOS does not collect names, emails, locations or hardware details.

Request logs

Like most web services, requests are logged with the time, IP address, requested path, response code and browser user agent. The hosting providers, Vercel for the pages and Railway for the API, handle your requests and keep their own logs under their own policies.

What stays in your browser

Local storage holds your wallet address, your wallet name, your sign-in token (valid for 24 hours) and your wallpaper choice. AetherOS sets no cookies and uses no analytics, ads or third-party trackers.

Fonts

The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads.

Your wallet

AetherOS sees your public address and a signature. It never sees your private key or your seed phrase. Your wallet app has its own policies.

Keeping and removing data

Balances and shares are kept while the service runs or until they are reset. Pressing DISCONNECT in wallet.exe removes your token and wallet details from this browser. Server records stay, and they hold only the data listed above.

Changes to this policy

This policy can change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
