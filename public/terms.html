What AetherOS is

AetherOS is a web app. You connect a Solana wallet, press start, and your browser computes SHA-256 hashes in a background thread of the open tab. When it finds a hash that meets the target, the server checks it. Each accepted share adds credits to your balance. You can spend credits on an assistant that learns from your own text, and the leaderboard ranks wallets by shares mined.

Credits

Credits are kept by AetherOS and can be spent in assistant.exe. They are not a token, a coin or money. They cannot be bought, sold, exchanged, transferred or withdrawn. The reward per share, the daily limits, the difficulty and the assistant's prices are set by the server and can change at any time.

The assistant

assistant.exe uses Claude, a model made by Anthropic. In TEACH you give it text, Claude reads it once and writes a profile of its voice and knowledge. In CHAT your assistant answers from that profile. A message and a profile each cost the credits shown in the window. Credits are taken when you send and returned when no answer comes back.

A wallet can use the assistant 24 hours after its first sign-in to AetherOS, while it holds at least 0.01 SOL on Solana. AetherOS reads that balance and never moves it. Each wallet has a daily limit of messages and profiles, shown in the window and reset at 00:00 UTC. The assistant also has a monthly capacity shared by everyone. When it is used up, the assistant pauses until 00:00 UTC on the 1st of the next month.

Replies are written by a model and can be wrong, so check anything that matters before you rely on it. Only upload text you have the right to use, and leave out passwords, keys and other people's personal data. Use of the assistant must follow Anthropic's usage policy. Requests that break it are declined, and the credits for them are returned.

Signing in

You sign one message with your wallet to prove you control the address. No transaction is sent and no fee is charged. AetherOS never asks for a token approval, a transfer or your seed phrase. Never share your seed phrase with anyone.

Fair use

The intent is one person and one wallet. Scripts, modified clients, or many wallets used to inflate credits or to use the assistant beyond its limits are not allowed, and entries that break this can be reset or removed from the leaderboard without notice. Each wallet can earn up to the daily limit shown in miner.exe. The limit resets at 00:00 UTC.

Your computer

Mining uses your processor while it runs. Press STOP or close the tab to end it. INTENSITY sets how much of each moment is spent hashing.

The service

AetherOS can change, pause or stop at any time. Balances, rankings and assistant profiles are stored on a single server and can be lost or reset.

Changes to these terms

These terms can change. The date at the top shows the latest version. Using AetherOS after a change means you accept it.
