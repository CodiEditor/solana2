What AetherOS is

AetherOS is a web app. You connect a Solana wallet, press start, and your browser computes SHA-256 hashes in a background thread of the open tab. When it finds a hash that meets the target, the server checks it. Each accepted share adds credits to your balance, and the leaderboard ranks wallets by credits.

Credits

Credits are a score kept by AetherOS. They are not a token, a coin or money. They cannot be bought, sold, exchanged, transferred or withdrawn, and nothing in AetherOS promises that credits will be usable for anything. The reward per share, the daily limit and the difficulty are set by the server and can change at any time.

Signing in

You sign one message with your wallet to prove you control the address. No transaction is sent and no fee is charged. AetherOS never asks for a token approval, a transfer or your seed phrase. Never share your seed phrase with anyone.

Fair use

The intent is one person and one wallet. Scripts, modified clients, or many wallets used to inflate credits are not allowed, and entries that break this can be reset or removed from the leaderboard without notice. Each wallet can earn up to the daily limit shown in miner.exe. The limit resets at 00:00 UTC.

Your computer

Mining uses your processor while it runs. Press STOP or close the tab to end it. INTENSITY sets how much of each moment is spent hashing.

The service

AetherOS can change, pause or stop at any time. Balances and rankings are stored on a single server and can be lost or reset.

Changes to these terms

These terms can change. The date at the top shows the latest version. Using AetherOS after a change means you accept it.
